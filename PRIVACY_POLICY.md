# RuAli Privacy Policy

## Last Updated: October 2026

### Introduction
RuAli Premium ("App") is committed to protecting children's privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

### Age Compliance
This App is designed for children ages 3-12. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### Information We Collect

#### Child Information
- Profile name and avatar (stored locally)
- Grade level selection
- Learning progress and completed lessons
- Quiz scores and badges

#### Usage Data
- Lesson interactions (stored locally)
- Time spent in app
- Navigation patterns

#### Device Information
- Device type and OS version
- App version

### How We Use Information
- To provide personalized learning paths
- To track progress and display achievements
- To improve the learning experience
- To comply with legal obligations

### Data Storage
- **Primary Storage**: AsyncStorage (local device storage)
- **No Cloud Sync**: All data remains on the device
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: Your data is not shared with external services

### Parental Rights
Parents can:
- View child progress through the Parent Dashboard
- Request data deletion by contacting support
- Opt out of non-essential data collection

### Security
- All data is encrypted locally
- No transmission to external servers
- Regular security audits

### Advertising
- No third-party advertising
- No behavioral tracking
- No data sold to advertisers
