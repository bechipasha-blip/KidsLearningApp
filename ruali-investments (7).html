Audits, in depth

Built on the checklist your team already trusts

RuALi's audit tool doesn't reinvent the checklist — it reads the same categories forecourt teams have signed off on for years. Attendants tick as normal on the daily, weekly, and monthly sheets; the AI reads them and turns the result into a same-day report.

Forecourt

Daily / Weekly
  • Pumps, OPTs and air hose all working
  • Driveway, canopy and signage clean and in good order
  • Pricing board correct, rust free and digits displaying

Shop

Daily / Weekly
  • Chillers, fridges and paypoints all working
  • Floors, shelves and paypoint equipment clean
  • Stock fully packed with correct pricing labels

Staff Areas

Daily / Weekly
  • Change rooms, lockers and showers clean and in order
  • Correct uniform, badges and safety shoes worn
  • No eating, smoking or gum on forecourt or shop

Toilets

Daily + Duty Schedule
  • Odour-free, sanitised, fully supplied facilities
  • Hourly on-duty sign-off schedule, shift by shift
  • SHE bin and extractor checks logged

Monthly Deep Clean

Monthly
  • Canopy, driveway and landscaping deep-cleaned
  • Aircon, storerooms and paypoint areas serviced
  • All equipment fixed or replaced, paint touch-ups done

Dealer Instructions

Weekly
  • Manager-to-supervisor instructions logged daily
  • Attendance and sign-off tracked across the week
  • Full trail ready for HSEQ and compliance review

Categories reflect the standard Cleanliness Confirmed daily, weekly, and monthly checklist framework used across forecourt, shop, staff, toilet, and dealer sign-off sheets.
